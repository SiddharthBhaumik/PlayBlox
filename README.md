# PlayBlox 🧸

Welcome to PlayBlox—a cheerful little playground for getting together with friends. Our goal is to bring simple, friendly multiplayer games into one colourful place, whether you're on a phone or a computer.

## What you can do

- **Create a party:** Choose a nickname and give your party a name.
- **Invite friends:** Share a party invite link or code.
- **Join a party:** Enter a friend's invite code to get ready to play.
- **Discover games:** Find games in the PlayBlox playroom as they become available.

## Current status

PlayBlox is under construction. The website currently offers a preview of the party lobby and its create/join interactions. Party codes are not yet connected to real online rooms, and friends cannot yet join the same live session. Playable games and real-time multiplayer are planned for future updates.

## Visit PlayBlox

Once the site is published, visit the PlayBlox website using the link provided by its owner.
